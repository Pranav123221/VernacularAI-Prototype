Chapter: Numbers 1 to 10
Grade: 1
Subject: Mathematics

Children learn to recognize numbers from 1 to 10 and say them in order. Counting means matching one number word to one object. When we count a group, the last number we say tells us how many objects are in the group.

We can count familiar things such as seeds, stones, sticks, fruits, or pencils. The number 1 means one object. The number 2 means two objects. The number 3 means three objects. The numbers continue in order: 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, and 10.

A number line helps us see order. The number after 3 is 4. The number before 6 is 5. We can compare small groups by counting each group. A group with more objects has a bigger number.

Teacher prompt: Encourage children to touch or move each object once while saying one number word. Ask children to show the number of objects with their fingers.

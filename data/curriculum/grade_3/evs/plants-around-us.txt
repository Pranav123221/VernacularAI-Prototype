Chapter: Plants Around Us
Grade: 3
Subject: Environmental Studies

Plants grow in many places. Some plants are herbs with soft stems, while others have hard woody stems. Trees, shrubs, herbs, climbers, and creepers are plants we can notice around us.

People use plants for food, medicine, shade, wood, and many other things. We should care for plants by giving them water, protecting young plants, and avoiding harm to living branches.

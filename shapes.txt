Chapter: Shapes Around Us
Grade: 1
Subject: Mathematics

We see shapes around us. A circle is round like a plate. A square has four equal sides like a small tile. A triangle has three sides. We can find and name shapes in objects we use every day.

Children can draw shapes with a finger in the air, trace them on paper, and look for the same shape in their classroom. Talking about sides and corners helps children notice how shapes are different.
